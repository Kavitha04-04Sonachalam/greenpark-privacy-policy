Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Green Park School PBLR ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting the privacy of parents, students, and users of our application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Green Park School PBLR mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

The App may collect and process the following information:

  • Parent/Guardian Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Student Information
  • Profile Information
  • Academic Information
  • Student Marks and Results
  • Attendance Records
  • Fee Details
  • School Notifications and Announcements
  • Profile Photos uploaded by users
  • Images uploaded through the application

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used to:

  • Authenticate users and provide secure access
  • Display student academic information
  • Provide attendance, marks, and fee details
  • Deliver notifications and school announcements
  • Enable profile management and photo uploads
  • Improve communication between the school and parents

3. Camera Access

The App may request access to the device camera to allow users to capture and upload profile photos. Camera access is used only for the intended functionality within the application.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. All communication between the application and backend services is transmitted securely using HTTPS.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only:

  • With authorized school administrators for educational purposes
  • When required by applicable law or legal process

6. Children's Privacy

The application is intended for parents and guardians of students enrolled at Green Park School. Student information is accessible only to authorized users associated with the student.

7. Data Retention

User information is retained only as long as necessary to provide school-related services and comply with applicable legal requirements.

8. User Rights

Users may contact the school administration regarding:

  • Access to their information
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Requests related to account management and data updates

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with the revised effective date.
